📖 Introduction

The Clinical Trial Risk Monitor & Protocol Deviation Detector is an AI-powered healthcare web application developed for the IBM BoB AI Innovation Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement P1.

Clinical trials are the cornerstone of evidence-based medicine. However, managing compliance across hundreds of patients at dozens of research sites is an enormous challenge. Every missed visit, wrong dose, or banned co-medication represents a protocol deviation that must be detected, documented, classified, and reported to regulatory authorities.

This project automates the entire deviation detection-to-CAPA pipeline using AI, reducing the time required for clinical research associates (CRAs) from days to seconds.

🎯 Problem Statement

Clinical trials involve thousands of patients across multiple research sites. Ensuring every patient follows the approved protocol (visit schedule, dosing, co-medications, lab tests) is critical for:

  • Patient Safety: Deviations can harm participants.
  • Data Integrity: Deviations compromise trial results.
  • Regulatory Compliance: FDA requires all deviations to be logged and reported per 21 CFR Part 312 and ICH E6 GCP.
  • Site Performance: High-risk sites must be identified early to prevent audit failures.
The Challenge: Traditional manual monitoring discovers deviations weeks after they occur, inconsistently classifies severity, and takes days to produce CAPA reports — leading to audit failures, trial delays, and millions in wasted investment.

CAPA reports take days to prepare manually — delaying regulatory submissions.

🤖 Proposed AI Solution

Our AI-powered solution addresses every limitation of the existing system through a fully automated, rule-based AI engine that applies ICH E6 GCP standards to every patient record in real time.

1
Real-time Detection

AI analyzes every patient record instantly — deviations detected same day they occur.

2
Standardized Classification

All deviations classified consistently using ICH E6 GCP rules — no human subjectivity.

3
Risk Scoring

Weighted 0–100 risk score per patient and site, providing clear audit priority rankings.

4
One-click CAPA Reports

AI generates full CAPA reports with root cause, corrective actions, and follow-up timelines in seconds.

5
Site-level Dashboard

Aggregated site risk rankings — always FDA-audit ready.

⚙️ AI Workflow

The AI pipeline follows five sequential steps for every patient record:

INPUT: Patient Visit Record
       {id, name, site, visitDate, scheduledDate,
        doseGiven, expectedDose, comed,
        labCompleted, docComplete}
          ↓
STEP 1: Visit Timing Analysis
        → |visitDate - scheduledDate| > 7 days → Minor
        → |visitDate - scheduledDate| > 14 days → Major
          ↓
STEP 2: Dose Deviation Check
        → ratio = doseGiven / expectedDose
        → ratio ≥ 2 or ≤ 0.5 → Major
        → other mismatch → Minor
          ↓
STEP 3: Co-medication Screen
        → comed in BANNED_MEDS list → Major
          ↓
STEP 4: Lab Test Audit
        → labCompleted === "No" → Major
          ↓
STEP 5: Documentation Check
        → docComplete === "No" → Administrative
          ↓
OUTPUT: Risk Score = Σ(deviation weights), capped at 100
        Category: Low(0-30) / Medium(31-60) / High(61-80) / Critical(81-100)
        CAPA: {rootCause, corrective, preventive, responsible, followUp}

🧠 AI Logic Detail

Risk Scoring Weights

const WEIGHTS = {
  majorDeviation:     25,   // Base weight for any major
  minorDeviation:     10,   // Base weight for any minor
  adminDeviation:      5,   // Base weight for admin
  missedVisit:        15,   // Visit timing breach
  wrongDose:          30,   // Dose error weight
  bannedMed:          35,   // Highest weight — patient safety
  missingCriticalLab: 20,   // Missing required lab test
  missingDoc:          5    // Documentation gap
};
// Risk Score = Σ(applicable weights), min(total, 100)

🏆 Conclusion

The ClinicalAI Monitor demonstrates how AI can transform clinical trial compliance from a reactive, error-prone manual process into a proactive, real-time automated system.

By implementing ICH E6 GCP rules as a deterministic AI engine, we achieve 100% consistency in deviation classification — eliminating the subjective variability that plagues manual monitoring.

The system reduces CAPA generation time from days to seconds, provides site-level risk visibility for proactive FDA audit preparation, and delivers actionable AI recommendations that CRAs can act on immediately.

Built entirely with vanilla JavaScript and HTML/CSS with no external frameworks, the application is lightweight, fast, and deployable anywhere — demonstrating that powerful AI solutions don't require heavy infrastructure.
