Workflow

How the AI Works

Our five-step AI pipeline processes patient records in real time and produces compliance-ready outputs.

1

Data Ingestion

Patient visit records are ingested including visit dates, dosages, co-medications, and lab results.

2

Rule-Based Deviation Detection

Each record is checked against ICH E6 protocol rules — visit windows, dose thresholds, banned medications.

3

Severity Classification

Detected deviations are classified as Major, Minor, or Administrative using GCP-defined criteria.

4

Risk Score Computation

A weighted algorithm calculates a 0–100 risk score per patient and aggregates it at the site level.

5

CAPA Report Generation

AI generates detailed CAPA reports with corrective actions, preventive measures, and follow-up timelines.
